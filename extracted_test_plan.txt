Feature Summary
Smart Geofence Alert adds a new "Smart" geofence mode to alert configuration. Instead of selecting individual trailers, a manager selects one or more existing alert configurations; the in-scope trailer set is the union of trailers across those configurations. When an in-scope trailer crosses the geofence boundary (entry or exit), the system automatically captures a snapshot of that trailer's active alerts, notifies configured recipients on every crossing, and exposes a per-location alert health report.
